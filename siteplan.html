Mangaldan Chamber of Commerce

Supporting Local Businesses and Community Growth

Site plan by Lehi Gomez

Site Purpose

This website will serve as a resource hub for businesses in the Town of Mangaldan. The site will provide information on local events, host networking opportunities, create business directories, and encourage local shopping. It aims to promote local economic growth, support local businesses, and foster a sense of community between the businesses.

Target Market

Business owners and patrons in the town of Mangaldan and surrounding areas.

Site Goals

  • Improve member engagement by providing valuable resources and networking opportunities.
  • Attract new businesses and encourage economic development in the community.
  • Improve the visibility and reputation of the chamber as a trusted authority in local business matters.

User Personas

MS

Small Business Owner

Maria Santos, 38

Sari-sari Store Owner

Maria owns a small sari-sari store in Mangaldan, Pangasinan. She wants to expand her business and attract more customers. She is looking for information about business programs, local networking opportunities, and resources that can help small business owners improve their businesses.

JM

Aspiring Entrepreneur

John Mark Dela Cruz, 26

Young Entrepreneur

John Mark is a young resident of Mangaldan who plans to start his own food business. He wants to learn about business opportunities, local market demands, permits, and available support for new entrepreneurs. He visits the chamber website to find useful information and connect with established business owners in the community.

AR

Local Resident and Consumer

Ana Reyes, 32

Public School Teacher

Ana is a resident of Mangaldan who wants to support local business in her community. She visits the chamber website to discover nearby shops, restaurants, services, and upcoming community events. She prefers finding reliable local business information in one convenient place.

Scenarios

Maria Santos

A small business owner in Mangaldan wants to grow her sari-sari store and attract more customers. She visits the Chamber of Commerce website to learn about membership benefits, business networking opportunities, and programs that support small local business.

John Mark Dela Cruz

A young resident of Mangaldan plans to start a small food business but does not know where to begin. He visits the website to find information about local business opportunities, entrepreneurship resources, and upcoming business seminars or workshops.

Ana Reyes

A local resident wants to support business within Mangaldan instead of always shopping in nearby cities. She visits the website to explore the business directory, discover local shops and restaurants, and find upcoming community events.

SEO Plan

  • Use relevant keywords in site descriptions, content, and blog posts.
  • Verify the site on Google's Business Profile.
  • Get inbound links or backlinks from all member businesses to improve rankings.
  • Embed Google Analytics in all site pages.
